About

I work on the physical security of cryptographic implementations. Devices leak secrets through power use and electromagnetic signals. Attackers can also inject faults to make them fail. I find these weaknesses and help make products secure against them.

I am a Staff Product Security Analyst at PQShield. I help make sure post-quantum cryptography implementations are secure against side-channel analysis (SCA) and fault injection (FI).

From 2016 to 2025 I worked at Riscure, now part of Keysight. There I evaluated secure elements, SoCs and roots of trust against SCA and FI attacks.

I received my PhD in computer science from the University of Luxembourg in 2015. My thesis was on provably secure countermeasures against side-channel attacks. It gave the first method to convert between Boolean and arithmetic masking that is secure against attacks of any order.

Research

  • SCA countermeasures. Masking, shuffling and other ways to protect cryptographic code against SCA. This includes secure and fast conversion between Boolean and arithmetic masking, and higher-order masking.
  • Fault injection. Voltage and EM fault attacks. How clock frequency affects faults. Using machine learning to find good fault settings automatically.
  • Post-quantum cryptography. Protecting new post-quantum algorithms against SCA and FI.
